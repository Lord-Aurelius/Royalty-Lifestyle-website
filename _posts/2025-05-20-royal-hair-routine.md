Healthy, lustrous hair doesn't happen by accident. It's the result of consistent care, the right products, and knowing when to leave things to the professionals. Here's the routine our stylists at Royalty swear by.

## Daily Habits That Make a Difference

Start with a silk or satin pillowcase. Cotton pulls moisture from your hair overnight, leading to breakage and dryness. Satin keeps your hair smooth and hydrated while you sleep.

Detangle gently, always from the ends upward. Never drag a brush from the roots down — this causes unnecessary breakage, especially on natural hair textures.

## Weekly Treatments

Once a week, apply a deep conditioning mask and leave it on for at least 20 minutes under a warm towel. This replaces moisture lost to heat styling, the sun, and washing.

A scalp massage with a light oil — jojoba or argan work beautifully — stimulates circulation and encourages healthy growth.

## When to Come Back to Us

Even with the best home routine, professional trimming every 6–8 weeks keeps split ends from travelling up the hair shaft. Come in for a treatment whenever your hair feels particularly dry or dull — our team will assess and treat the root cause.

Beautiful hair is a partnership between you and your stylist. We handle the expertise; you handle the consistency.
