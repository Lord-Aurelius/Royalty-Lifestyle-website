Your skin is talking to you. Dullness, breakouts, and premature lines are often signals that something in your routine needs adjusting. Our skincare therapists at Royalty share the five mistakes they see most often.

## 1. Washing Your Face With Hot Water

Hot water strips your skin's natural oils and disrupts the moisture barrier. Use lukewarm water instead — it cleanses effectively without the damage.

## 2. Skipping SPF on Cloudy Days

UV rays penetrate clouds. If you're only applying SPF on sunny days, your skin is accumulating damage the rest of the time. Make SPF a daily habit, not a seasonal one.

## 3. Over-Exfoliating

More is not better with exfoliation. Scrubbing too often damages the skin barrier and causes redness, sensitivity, and breakouts. Once or twice a week is enough for most skin types.

## 4. Using Too Many Active Ingredients at Once

Layering retinol, AHAs, vitamin C, and niacinamide all at once is a recipe for irritation. Introduce one active ingredient at a time and give your skin two to four weeks to adjust.

## 5. Not Getting Professional Facials

Home skincare can only do so much. Professional facials reach deeper, remove congestion you can't address at home, and allow a trained eye to catch early signs of skin issues. We recommend a professional facial every 4–6 weeks.
