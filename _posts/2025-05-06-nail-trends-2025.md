Nail art has never been more exciting. 2025 is a year of contrasts — understated elegance sitting alongside bold, expressive designs. Here's what our nail artists are seeing most requested at Royalty this season.

## Quiet Luxury Neutrals

Soft taupes, creamy whites, and warm nudes are having a major moment. These shades photograph beautifully and work across every skin tone. They're the "no-makeup makeup" of the nail world.

## Chrome and Mirror Finishes

High-shine chrome nails — especially in gold, rose gold, and gunmetal — are everywhere. The reflective finish catches light beautifully and looks incredibly premium. Our nail artists can achieve this with chrome powder over gel.

## Negative Space Designs

Leaving portions of the natural nail exposed as part of the design creates a modern, graphic look. It's minimalist but striking — especially with geometric cutouts and thin line art.

## Glazed Donut Nails

The glazed nail trend is still going strong. A sheer, milky base with a hint of shimmer gives nails a soft, dimensional glow that looks effortlessly elegant.

## French with a Twist

The classic French tip is being reimagined with coloured tips, reverse French designs, and double-line variations. Ask our nail artists for a modern French during your next visit.
